About Maison Aurelle

A small house making bold gold-tone jewelry — sculptural pieces meant to be worn often and stacked freely.

Our story

Fewer pieces, made properly

We started with a simple frustration: statement jewelry that was either flimsy or priced like fine jewelry, and rarely honest about what it was made of. Maison Aurelle is our answer — a focused edit of sculptural gold-tone pieces we would want to wear ourselves, priced plainly.

Every piece is chosen for proportion and presence: cuffs that sit high on the wrist, bangles that stack without clatter, earrings with volume but no weight. If a detail does not earn its place, it goes.

We are a small team, and we would rather do a few things well than many things quickly. That means limited quantities, careful finishing and a reply from a real person when you write to us.

Craft & materials

What goes into each piece

Our pieces are 18K gold PVD over stainless steel: a durable gold-tone finish bonded to a nickel-free, hypoallergenic steel base. Stone-set rings use natural stones such as tiger eye and onyx, or cubic zirconia; natural stones vary slightly piece to piece.

PVD plating is harder-wearing than traditional gold plating and water-resistant for everyday wear. It is not solid gold: keep it away from perfume, lotion and chlorine and it will keep its colour for years.

Each piece is inspected by hand before it is boxed. Materials, dimensions and care are listed on every product page so you know exactly what you are buying.
