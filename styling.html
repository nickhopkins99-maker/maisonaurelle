Styling & Care

How to wear the six stories together, and how to keep every piece shining.

Styling FAQ

Trends

Is chunky gold still in style?

Yes. Bold, sculptural gold with an '80s-luxury feel is one of the strongest jewelry moods of the year. Start with one Molten Tide cuff or a pair of Hammered Moon hoops, then build.

How many necklaces should I layer?

Two or three. Pair one short chain with one Lost Treasury coin a few inches lower, so each piece has room to show.

Can I mix collections?

That is the point. Every piece shares the same warm 18K tone, so a Frangipani stud, a Wanderer's ring and a Grand bangle all belong together.

Everyday care

Can I wear it in water?

The 18K PVD finish over stainless steel resists water and tarnish, so a splash or a shower is fine. Take pieces off before pools and hot tubs, since chlorine and salt wear any plating faster.

How do I keep the shine?

Put jewelry on after lotion, perfume and sunscreen. Wipe each piece with a soft, dry cloth after wearing.

How should I store it?

Keep pieces apart in a lined box or pouch, so bangles and cuffs don't scratch each other.
