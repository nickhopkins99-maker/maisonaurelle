Who we are

Maison Aurelle is operated by [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS]. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us about privacy at [EMAIL].

Data we collect

  • Contact and shipping details you enter at checkout or in the contact form: name, email, phone (optional), shipping address and any order notes.
  • Order contents: the pieces, quantities and prices in your order request.
  • Newsletter email, only if you sign up.
  • Device and usage data (pages viewed, approximate location from IP, browser type) only if you accept analytics in the cookie banner.

We do not collect payment card details on this site.

Cookies & analytics

  • Your shopping bag and your cookie choice are stored in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us until you place an order.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4) loads only after you choose “Accept analytics”. If you decline, no analytics script is loaded and no analytics cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.
  • We use no advertising trackers, retargeting pixels or data brokers.

Payment processors

We do not collect or store card data on this site. When payment is arranged it is processed by [PAYMENT PROCESSOR] under its own privacy policy; we receive confirmation of payment, not your card number.
