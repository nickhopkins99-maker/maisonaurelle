Shipping

Orders ship within 7–14 business days of payment being confirmed. Shipping is complimentary on orders over $250; below that, a flat rate of $15 applies.

  • Every parcel is insured and tracked door to door.
  • Orders over $1,000 require a signature on delivery.
  • We currently ship to the countries listed at checkout. Duties or import taxes outside the United States are the recipient’s responsibility.

Returns

You may return a piece within 30 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method, provided it is unworn and in its original packaging with all documentation.

  • Pieces sized or customised to your specification are final sale.
  • Write to our store inbox with your order number to start a return; we send a prepaid, insured label.
  • Refunds are issued within 5 business days of the piece arriving back with us.

Exchanges & sizing

Ring sizes and bangle diameters are confirmed with you before shipping. If a piece arrives and the size is wrong, we exchange an unworn piece for another size within 30 days of delivery.

For any other exchange, return the original piece under the policy above and place a new order.

Care

  • Store pieces separately in the pouch or box provided to avoid scratching.
  • Remove jewelry before swimming, bathing, exercising or applying lotions and perfume.
  • Our pieces are 18K gold PVD over stainless steel: the finish is water-resistant, but perfume, lotion, hairspray and chlorine will dull it over time. Put jewelry on last.
  • Clean with a soft, dry cloth. For stubborn marks use warm water and a drop of mild soap, then dry fully. No ultrasonic cleaners or polishing compounds.
  • Natural stones (tiger eye, onyx, malachite) can scratch and vary in pattern; keep them away from hard knocks.
  • The steel base is nickel-free and hypoallergenic; the plating is not solid gold.

Warranty

Every piece is covered for one year from delivery against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. This does not cover normal wear, loss, accidental damage, or work carried out by a third party. Contact us with photos and your order number and we will arrange repair or replacement.
